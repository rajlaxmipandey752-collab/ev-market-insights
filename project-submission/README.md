## Project Overview

Electric Vehicle Market Performance & Insights is a data analytics and visualization project focused on exploring the electric vehicle industry through Tableau. It aims to transform EV market data into meaningful visual insights that help users understand manufacturer performance, vehicle categories, battery technologies, driving range, customer satisfaction, and geographical manufacturing patterns.

The project supports data-driven decision-making for electric vehicle manufacturers, automotive business intelligence professionals, market strategists, and sustainable mobility researchers. Through interactive dashboards and a Tableau Story, users can explore market trends, compare vehicle characteristics, identify competitive patterns, and investigate opportunities in the evolving electric vehicle market.

## Project Scenarios
